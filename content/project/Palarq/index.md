The Palarq project -**Isotopic characterization of Iberian and Breton sillimanite**- is funded by the Fundación Palarq (Convocatoria de analíticas 2025-2026. 2026-2027).

This project analyzes a representative set of geological and archaeological samples collected during the [ARPA]({{< relref "/project/ARPA" >}}) and [ANDALUSIL]({{< relref "/project/ANDALUSIL" >}}) projects, covering more than fifty locations in Spain and France. Their isotopic characterization will provide higher-resolution data, allowing a more effective discrimination between potential sources of supply.
